I'm a senior engineer and consultant based in Bengaluru, India, with 11+ years of experience designing, automating and running production cloud platforms across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.

My foundation is Linux, build and release engineering, and infrastructure automation. On top of it, I've grown into designing complete DevOps, platform and reliability solutions for enterprise workloads. I also bring Agentic AI, LLMs and RAG into cloud operations.

As a consultant, I get up to speed on a team's platform quickly and design solutions that fit it. I leave behind infrastructure that is automated, observable, secure and easy for the team to own.
